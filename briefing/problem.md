# Problem Definition

## Research Question

Given observations of strategic behavior that are assumed to be an equilibrium of an unknown parametric game, recover game parameters for which the observed behavior is rational. In the scored static benchmarks, the tasks are to recover buyer budgets in synthetic Cobb-Douglas or linear Fisher markets, recover both budgets and Cobb-Douglas type shares in a joint Fisher setting, and recover marginal production costs in synthetic Cournot and Bertrand duopoly games from observed equilibrium quantities or prices.

## Why It Matters

Recovering payoff or market parameters from equilibrium behavior supports counterfactual prediction, preference estimation, and economic model calibration when rewards or utilities are not directly observed.

## Success Criteria

A successful solution estimates parameters close to the data-generating parameters and keeps the observed behavior close to equilibrium under those estimates. Parameter recovery alone is insufficient if the recovered game assigns high exploitability to the observed behavior.
