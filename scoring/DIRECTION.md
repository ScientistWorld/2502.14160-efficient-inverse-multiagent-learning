# Research Direction

Stay within inverse game theory for equilibrium rationalization: infer market or payoff parameters from observed equilibrium behavior so that the behavior is rational under the inferred game. The open problem in this gym is inference from *sampled, noisy* equilibrium behavior (`fisher_cd_noisy_budget_recovery`): obtaining more accurate parameters from the same observations while keeping the true equilibrium unexploitable. Improvements should come from better inverse-equilibrium formulations, estimators or optimization that work from the observations. They should not come from supervised regression on true parameters, from reading ground truth, or from editing the benchmark data. Regressions on the exact-observation experiments (Cobb-Douglas and linear Fisher budgets, Cournot cost) are penalized, so a new method must keep solving them.
