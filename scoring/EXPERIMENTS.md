# Experiments

## fisher_cd_budget_recovery

This experiment asks whether a method can recover hidden buyer budgets from observed equilibrium allocations and prices in small Cobb-Douglas Fisher markets. It measures both parameter accuracy and whether the observed behavior remains close to equilibrium under the recovered parameters.

## cournot_cost_recovery

This experiment asks whether a method can recover a hidden marginal production cost from observed equilibrium quantities in small duopoly markets. It measures both parameter accuracy and whether the observed quantities remain close to equilibrium under the recovered cost.

## bertrand_cost_recovery

This experiment asks whether a method can recover a hidden marginal production cost from observed equilibrium prices in small duopoly markets. It measures both parameter accuracy and whether the observed prices remain close to equilibrium under the recovered cost.

## fisher_linear_budget_recovery

This experiment asks whether a method can recover hidden buyer budgets from observed equilibrium allocations and prices in small linear-utility Fisher markets. It measures both parameter accuracy and whether the observed behavior remains close to equilibrium under the recovered budgets.

## fisher_leontief_budget_recovery

This experiment asks whether a method can recover hidden buyer budgets from observed equilibrium allocations and prices in small Leontief-utility Fisher markets. It measures both parameter accuracy and whether the observed behavior remains close to equilibrium under the recovered budgets.
